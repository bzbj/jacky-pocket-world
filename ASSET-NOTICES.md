# Artwork provenance

- The home background preserves Jacky Lin and the reading penguin from the
  owner's personal pocket-card design. The penguin was generated for that
  prototype. The visible name is demo content; no contact URL, personal QR,
  network settings or avatar card is included.
- The three small characters are original pixel drawings expressed as rectangles
  in `tools/build_assets.py`: a blue robot, an orange robot and a blue whale.
  They preserve the three-character composition and fixed positions of the
  original design. They replace the original Codex, Claude and DeepSeek mascot
  pictures. No desktop-app spritesheet or third-party logo image is included or
  required by the build.
- The WeChat placeholder, settings layout and small TV robot are also drawn by
  code. The placeholder contains no QR finder patterns or encoded data.
- No font file is bundled. Optional local fonts are used only during asset
  generation. Compiling uses the already-generated RGB565 arrays.
